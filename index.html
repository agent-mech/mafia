How to install
  1. Tap Download APK and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back and tap Install.
  3. Open Mafia. Allow notifications and nearby devices when you host.

The iPhone app is on its way to the App Store. Until then, iPhone players can join any game from Safari below. No app needed.

Join in the browser

Play in the browser, no app needed. The game runs on the host's iPhone.

How to join

  1. Connect to the same Wi-Fi as the host, or to the host's personal hotspot.
  2. On the host's iPhone, find the Address under Room in the lobby, for example 192.168.1.23:47777.
  3. Type it above and tap Join. Or skip all this and scan the host's QR code with your camera.

Hosting

Hosting needs the Mafia app. Everyone else can join with the app or from this page, on any phone.

Games run on your local network. This page never sees them.
